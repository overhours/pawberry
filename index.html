02 · Battle

Real-time fights you actually play

Pick five cats, mind your elements, then step in at the right moment.

  1. Specials. Each cat charges a special. Fire it from the top bar or press 1–5. Two close together make a combo.
  2. Block. When a boss charges a big attack, hit Block right at the end to soften the blow.
  3. Focus. Click any enemy and the whole team goes after it.

    09 · Help

    Questions

    Is Pawberry free?

    Yes. It's free to download and play. Gems are earned by playing: battles, quests, mini-games, the daily login gift and more.

    Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

    That warning appears because the game isn't code-signed yet, not because anything is wrong. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only need to do it once.

    How do updates work?

    The installed game checks for new versions every few minutes. When one is out, an Update! button appears at the top. Click it, and Pawberry downloads it, shows a small installing window and reopens on the new version. The portable version links you to the newest download instead.

    Will I lose my save when I update?

    No. Saves live in your Windows user folder, separate from the game files. You can also export a save from Settings to back it up or move it to another computer.

    What do the leaderboards share?

    Nothing, unless you join from the Ranks screen. Then your profile name and six stats are shown: rebirths, hours played, gems earned, bosses beaten, coins earned and fish caught. You can stop sharing any time.

    Does it work on Mac or Linux? What about phones?

    Linux, yes! Grab the AppImage (it updates itself) or the .deb. A Mac version is on the way. There's no phone version yet.
