    Questions

    Is Pawberry free?

    Yes! It's free to download and play. Gems are earned by playing: battles, quests, mini-games, the daily login gift and more.

    Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

    That warning appears because the game isn't code-signed yet, not because anything is wrong. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only need to do it once.

    How do updates work?

    The installed game checks for new versions every few minutes. When one is out, an Update! button appears at the top. Click it, and Pawberry downloads the update, shows a small installing window and reopens on the new version. The portable version links you to the newest download instead.

    Where are my saves? Will I lose them when I update?

    Saves live in your Windows user folder, separate from the game files, so updating never touches them. You can also export a save from Settings to back it up or move it to another computer.

    What do the leaderboards share?

    Nothing unless you choose to join from the Ranks screen. Then your profile name and six stats are shown: rebirths, hours played, gems earned, bosses beaten, coins earned and fish caught. You can stop sharing any time.

    Is there a Mac or phone version?

    Not yet. Pawberry is Windows-only for now.
